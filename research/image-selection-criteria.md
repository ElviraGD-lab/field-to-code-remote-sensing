# Image selection criteria (written before the search)

Recorded on 10 November 2026, before any image was selected. Any later change is recorded below with the reason, and results are shown under both rules.

**Fire:** Bustantigo (Allande, Asturias), start 6 April 2026 (EFFIS, 01:30 local time).

**Area of interest (AOI):** the burnt area plus a 1 km buffer. All criteria are checked on the AOI, not on the whole scene. Scene cloud cover is used only to narrow the search.

## Criteria

1. Processing level: Sentinel-2 L2A.
2. Post-fire image: the first clear image after the fire is out; no smoke, no active fire.
3. Pre-fire image: the closest clear image before the fire started.
   - good: up to 2 weeks between the two images;
   - acceptable: up to 6 weeks;
   - more than 6 weeks: take the closest clear image, record it as a weakness and test its effect (chapter 7).
4. No clouds, cloud shadows or haze over the AOI, checked visually in true colour and SWIR (B12, B8A, B4).
5. No snow over the AOI.
6. Same relative orbit for both images if possible; otherwise record it.
7. No earlier burn on the AOI before the fire.

If no pair meets all criteria, the best available pair is used, the broken criterion is recorded and its effect is tested. Criteria are not relaxed silently.

## Image passport (recorded for each selected image)

Date and time; satellite (2A, 2B or 2C); relative orbit; tile; scene cloud cover; cloud cover over the AOI; sun elevation and azimuth; processing baseline.
